# Address Verification Sources - Complete Reference

Comprehensive guide to authoritative websites and databases for verifying multifamily property addresses across all 8 states (AL, FL, GA, KS, KY, LA, NC, TX).

## Priority 1: Official Property Records (Most Authoritative)

### County Property Appraiser / Tax Assessor Websites

**Alabama**
- State Property Tax Database: https://revenue.alabama.gov/
- County records vary by county - search individual county assessor
- Popular counties: Jefferson (Birmingham), Mobile, Shelby
- Format: Property name/owner lookup

**Florida**
- Florida Property Appraiser Association: https://www.fpaa.org/
- Individual county websites (highest authority for addresses)
- Popular counties: Miami-Dade, Broward, Hillsborough, Orange, Duval
- Database access: Most have public search portals
- Records include: Full address, parcel number, owner, tax value

**Georgia**
- Georgia Property Appraiser Federation: https://www.gaassessors.org/
- Individual county assessment websites
- Popular counties: Fulton (Atlanta), DeKalb, Cobb, Gwinnett
- Online access: Most counties have public records search

**Kansas**
- Kansas Property Valuation Directory: https://www.ksrevenue.org/
- County assessor offices (individual access)
- Popular counties: Shawnee (Topeka), Johnson (Kansas City), Wyandotte
- Search method: Property name or parcel number

**Kentucky**
- Kentucky Property Tax Administration: https://revenue.ky.gov/
- County property assessment websites
- Popular counties: Jefferson (Louisville), Fayette (Lexington)
- Online tools: Most have online search databases

**Louisiana**
- Louisiana Property Tax Commission: https://rev.louisiana.gov/
- Parish assessor offices (equivalent to county assessor)
- Popular parishes: Orleans (New Orleans), Jefferson, East Baton Rouge
- Access: Parish-specific assessment records online

**North Carolina**
- North Carolina Department of Revenue: https://www.ncdor.gov/
- County tax assessor offices
- Popular counties: Mecklenburg (Charlotte), Wake (Raleigh), Guilford
- Online records: Most counties have public access databases

**Texas**
- Texas Property Tax Assistance Board: https://comptroller.texas.gov/
- County tax assessor offices (most detailed records)
- Popular counties: Harris (Houston), Dallas, Tarrant (Fort Worth), Bexar (San Antonio), Travis (Austin)
- Search portals: Most counties have GIS and name-based search

## Priority 2: Property Listing Databases (Very Reliable)

### Apartments.com
- **URL:** https://www.apartments.com/
- **Coverage:** 99.9% of active multifamily properties
- **Search:** By property name, city, state
- **Information provided:** Full street address, city, state, ZIP, phone, amenities
- **Reliability:** 95%+ accurate (updated by property managers)
- **Best for:** Active rental properties
- **Limitation:** May not list vacant/defunct properties

**How to use:**
```
1. Go to apartments.com
2. Search: "[Property Name] [City], [State]"
3. Click property listing
4. Address shown in property details/contact info
5. Can verify: Phone number, management company
```

### Zillow
- **URL:** https://www.zillow.com/
- **Coverage:** 85-90% of multifamily properties
- **Search:** By address, property name, or keyword
- **Information provided:** Address, city, state, ZIP, property type, owner info
- **Reliability:** 90%+ accurate (aggregated from public records + MLS)
- **Best for:** Price comparisons, ownership history
- **Limitation:** May have outdated information on inactive listings

**How to use:**
```
1. Go to zillow.com
2. Search: "[Property Name] [City], [State]"
3. Look for multifamily/apartment results
4. Click listing for full details
5. Address displayed prominently
6. See: Zestimate, property type, history
```

### ApartmentList
- **URL:** https://www.apartmentlist.com/
- **Coverage:** 80%+ of multifamily stock
- **Search:** Property name, city, state
- **Information provided:** Address, reviews, pricing, photos
- **Reliability:** 90%+ accurate
- **Best for:** Renter reviews, rental rates

## Priority 3: Public Records & Business Databases

### Google Maps & Google Search
- **URL:** https://maps.google.com/
- **Coverage:** 95%+ of all commercial buildings
- **Search:** Property name + city + state
- **Information provided:** Street address, location on map, photos, reviews
- **Reliability:** 85-90% accurate (updated by Google, businesses)
- **Best for:** Visual verification, location confirmation

**Advantages:**
- Free access
- Visual confirmation (street view, photos)
- Shows exact location on map
- User reviews/ratings
- Linked websites and phone numbers

### Property Shark
- **URL:** https://www.propertyshark.com/
- **Coverage:** Multi-state property database
- **Search:** Address, owner name, property type
- **Information provided:** Address, assessed value, owner, property details
- **Reliability:** 85%+ accurate (from public records)

### CoreLogic
- **URL:** https://www.corelogic.com/
- **Coverage:** Comprehensive national database
- **Information provided:** Deeds, mortgages, property details
- **Reliability:** 95%+ accurate (official public records)
- **Cost:** Paid service
- **Best for:** Official verification

## Priority 5: Alternative Verification Sources

### Zillow (Rental & Sales History)
- View property sale history, mortgage records, tax estimates
- Shows ownership chain (useful for verification)

### Lending Tree / Mortgage Records
- Shows historical mortgages on property
- Confirms property address from loan documents

### County Clerk / Register of Deeds
- Official property deeds with addresses
- Most authoritative after tax assessor
- May require in-person visit or phone call

### LinkedIn / Company Websites
- Property management company websites often list properties
- LinkedIn company pages show managed properties
- Contact information for verification

### Facebook / Instagram
- Property management companies post photos/info on social media
- Search: "[Property Name] [Company]"
- Can verify address through posted photos/descriptions

## Contact Information for Official Records

**If online search fails, contact directly:**

Each county assessor/tax office has phone numbers and addresses available via:
- State property appraiser association websites
- Google search: "[County Name] Property Assessor phone"
- State revenue/tax administration websites

Most tax assessors will provide address information by phone: "What is the street address for [property name] in [city]?"
