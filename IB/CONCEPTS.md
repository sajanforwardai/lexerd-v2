## Core Valuation Methods

### 1. **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**
**What it is:** Value = sum of all future cash flows, discounted to present value

**Key components:**
- **Revenue projections** — Future growth estimates
- **Operating margins** — EBITDA/FCF conversion
- **Terminal value** — Value beyond projection period (usually 40-50% of total value)
- **WACC** — Discount rate reflecting risk and capital structure

**Use case:** Most rigorous method; heavily used in M&A

### 2. **Comparable Company Analysis ("Comps")**
**What it is:** Value based on multiples of similar public companies

**Key multiples:**
- **EV/Revenue** — Entire company value ÷ annual revenue
- **EV/EBITDA** — Entire company value ÷ earnings before tax/interest/depreciation/amortization
- **P/E Ratio** — Stock price ÷ earnings per share (equity only)

**Use case:** Market-based, reality-checks valuation; quick benchmarking

### 3. **Precedent Transactions**
**What it is:** Historical M&A multiples paid for similar companies

**Key metrics:**
- What did acquirers pay? (in multiples)
- What was the transaction rationale?
- What synergies were cited?

**Use case:** Realistic transaction pricing; deal precedent

### 4. **Discounted Dividend Model (DDM)**
**What it is:** Value of company = sum of all future dividends, discounted to present

**Formula:** 
```
Price = D₁/(r - g)
where:
D₁ = next dividend
r = required return
g = growth rate
```

**Use case:** Dividend-paying companies; mature, stable businesses

## Financial Concepts

### Valuation Multiples

**Enterprise Value (EV)**
- Total value of the company to all investors
- = Market cap + Net debt
- = Equity value + Debt - Cash

**EBITDA**
- Earnings before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization
- Proxy for cash generation
- Most common metric in M&A

**Revenue Multiple**
- EV ÷ Revenue
- Used when EBITDA not meaningful (pre-profit startups)
- Higher for growth companies

---

### Cost of Capital

**Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)**
```
WACC = (E/V × Re) + (D/V × Rd × (1-Tc))

where:
E = market value of equity
D = market value of debt
V = E + D
Re = cost of equity (using CAPM)
Rd = cost of debt (interest rate)
Tc = corporate tax rate
```

**Cost of Equity (CAPM)**
```
Re = Rf + β(Rm - Rf)

where:
Rf = risk-free rate
β = beta (stock volatility vs market)
Rm - Rf = market risk premium
```

## Key Financial Ratios & Metrics

### Profitability
- **Gross Margin** = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue
- **EBITDA Margin** = EBITDA / Revenue
- **Net Margin** = Net Income / Revenue

### Leverage
- **Net Debt** = Total Debt - Cash
- **Net Debt/EBITDA** = How many years of EBITDA to pay off net debt
- **Interest Coverage** = EBITDA / Interest Expense

### Growth
- **YoY Growth** = (Current Year - Prior Year) / Prior Year
- **CAGR** = Compound Annual Growth Rate
- **Market Share** = Company Revenue / Total Market Revenue

### Returns
- **ROE** = Net Income / Shareholders' Equity
- **ROIC** = NOPAT / Invested Capital (how efficiently capital is deployed)

## Investment Banking Terminology

**Pitch Book** — Presentation to prospective clients selling the bank's services and deal strategy

**Offering Memorandum (OM)** — Confidential document describing the company being sold to potential buyers

**Process** — Structured sale process (confidential information, NDA, management meetings, final bids)

**Seller's Advisor** — Bank hired by the owner/board to maximize value

**Buyer's Advisor** — Bank hired by acquirer to evaluate deals and negotiate

**Fairness Opinion** — Third-party analysis confirming transaction price is fair to shareholders

**Confidentiality Agreement (NDA)** — Legal agreement preventing disclosure of sensitive information

**Management Presentation** — Opportunity for buyers to meet company leadership and ask questions

**Bid** — Proposed purchase price from prospective buyer

**Synergies** — Cost savings or revenue benefits from combining two companies

## Common Mistakes (Learn From These)

### Valuation Errors
- ❌ Using outdated revenue projections
- ❌ Forgetting to include all adjustments (taxes, capex, working capital)
- ❌ Applying wrong discount rate for risk level
- ✅ Always stress-test assumptions and run sensitivity analysis

### Communication Errors
- ❌ Using jargon without explaining
- ❌ Presenting without context for client's situation
- ❌ Not backing up claims with numbers
- ✅ Explain the reasoning, show your work, connect to client needs

### Client Management Errors
- ❌ Promising certainty in uncertain situations
- ❌ Disagreeing with MD in front of client
- ❌ Missing deadlines or deliverables
- ✅ Under-promise and over-deliver; support your team publicly

### Analysis Errors
- ❌ Anchoring on first valuation (fixate on the number)
- ❌ Ignoring industry or macro trends
- ❌ Not questioning assumptions
- ✅ Challenge the numbers; understand the business; think independently

## Reading Financial Statements

### Income Statement
Shows profitability over a period
- **Revenue** — Top line (what the company sells)
- **Operating expenses** — Cost of doing business
- **EBITDA** — Operating profit before financing/taxes
- **Net Income** — Bottom line (what's left)

### Balance Sheet
Shows financial position at a point in time
- **Assets** — What the company owns
- **Liabilities** — What the company owes
- **Equity** — What owners have

### Cash Flow Statement
Shows actual cash movements
- **Operating Cash Flow** — Cash from business operations
- **Capex** — Capital expenditures (investments)
- **Free Cash Flow** — Operating cash flow minus capex (cash available for dividends/debt)
