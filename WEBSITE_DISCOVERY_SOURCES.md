# Website Discovery Sources - Complete Reference

Comprehensive guide to finding property websites and contact information across all 8 states (AL, FL, GA, KS, KY, LA, NC, TX).

---

## Priority 1: Direct Website Search (Fastest)

### Google Search
- **URL:** https://www.google.com/
- **Search:** "[Property Name] [City] [State] website"
- **Time:** 1-2 minutes per property
- **Reliability:** 85%+ (most properties have websites)
- **Best for:** Official property websites, management company sites
- **Tip:** Add "apartments" or "multifamily" to narrow results

**Example search:**
```
"Uptown Dallas" Dallas Texas website
"Park Place Apartments" Houston Texas official site
```

### Google Business Search
- **URL:** https://www.google.com/maps/
- **Search:** Property name + city + state
- **Information provided:** Website, phone, hours, reviews, location
- **Time:** 1-2 minutes per property
- **Reliability:** 80%+ (businesses update their Google Business listings)

---

## Priority 2: Listing Aggregators (Very Reliable)

### Apartments.com
- **URL:** https://www.apartments.com/
- **Coverage:** 99%+ of active multifamily properties
- **Search:** "[Property Name] [City], [State]"
- **Information provided:** Website link, phone number, email, photos, virtual tours
- **Reliability:** 95%+ (property managers maintain listings)
- **Best for:** Current phone numbers and official websites
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes per property

**How to use:**
```
1. Go to apartments.com
2. Search: "Property Name City, State"
3. Click property listing
4. See: Website link, phone, email, management contact
5. Note management company name
```

### ApartmentList
- **URL:** https://www.apartmentlist.com/
- **Coverage:** 80%+ of multifamily stock
- **Search:** Property name, city, state
- **Information provided:** Website, reviews, pricing, contact
- **Reliability:** 90%+
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes per property

### Zillow
- **URL:** https://www.zillow.com/
- **Coverage:** 85%+ of multifamily properties
- **Search:** Property name or address
- **Information provided:** Website, price, reviews, contact info
- **Reliability:** 85%+
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes per property

## Priority 3: Management Company Research

### Management Company Websites
**Strategy:** Find the property management company, then access their full portfolio

**How:**
1. Search: "[Property Name] property management company"
2. Find management company name
3. Visit management company website
4. Look up property in their portfolio
5. Get phone and website from listing

**Best sources for finding management companies:**
- Apartments.com (shows management company name)
- Property listing sites (management company often listed)
- Google Business listing (contact info)
- Property deed records (sometimes lists management)

**Time:** 5-10 minutes per property (requires research)
**Reliability:** 90%+ (management companies list all properties)

## Priority 4: Official Property Records

### County Property Appraiser Websites
- **Coverage:** 100% of properties (by law)
- **Information:** Deed info, owner contact, may list management company
- **Reliability:** 100% accurate (official records)
- **Time:** 5-15 minutes per property
- **Limitation:** Deed info may list prior owner/management company, not current

**State-specific:**
- **Alabama:** Individual county assessor websites
- **Florida:** County property appraiser websites (very detailed)
- **Georgia:** DeKalb, Fulton counties have excellent online systems
- **Kansas:** County assessor search
- **Kentucky:** County property valuation examiner
- **Louisiana:** Parish assessor websites
- **North Carolina:** County register of deeds
- **Texas:** County tax assessor GIS databases (most detailed statewide)

## Pro Tips for Fastest Website Discovery

**Time-saving strategies:**
1. **Open multiple tabs:** Have Apartments.com and Google open in tabs, switch between them
2. **Copy property name:** Paste property name into search boxes (faster than typing)
3. **Check Google Business first:** Look for business profile before searching general web
4. **Verify domain format:** Common patterns: `[propertyname]apartments.com`, `[propertyname]apts.com`, etc.
5. **Search management company:** If property site is down, management company site works
6. **Use results suggestions:** Google autocomplete often suggests official website in dropdown

**Batch workflow (optimal):**
1. Open Excel/CSV with all properties + search columns
2. Open 3 browser tabs: Apartments.com, Google Search, Notes
3. Process properties in city groups (all Houston properties together)
4. Estimated rate: 3-5 minutes per property
5. With 4 hours focused work: 48-80 properties

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't:**
- Assume property name = website domain (e.g., "Park Place" doesn't mean parkplace.com)
- Use outdated information from old business listings
- Rely on Facebook pages as primary website
- Forget to verify the city (many properties have similar names in different cities)
- Accept broken/outdated websites (keep searching)
- Record phone numbers without verifying they still work

✅ **DO:**
- Verify website is current (check for recent property updates)
- Look for "Contact Us" page to verify phone/email
- Record website URL exactly as it appears
- Cross-reference with Apartments.com to confirm property
- Note if website is down or outdated (may need manual follow-up)
