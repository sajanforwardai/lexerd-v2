================================================================================
SCHEMA DESIGN (9 ENTITY TYPES)
================================================================================

1. MarketRegime
   - Market conditions (bull, bear, high-vol, low-vol, etc.)
   - Attributes: characteristics, volatility_level, risk_profile

2. Strategy
   - Trading strategies (delta hedging, gamma scalping, vol arbitrage, etc.)
   - Attributes: description, risk_level, capital_requirement, time_horizon

3. Greeks
   - Option Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho)
   - Attributes: definition, formula, interpretation, risk_factor

4. VolSurface
   - Volatility surface characteristics and patterns
   - Attributes: characteristic, pattern, trading_implication

5. TradingOpportunity
   - Specific market misalignments and trading setups
   - Attributes: description, entry_criteria, exit_criteria, expected_return

6. Event
   - Market events (earnings, economic data, mergers, etc.)
   - Attributes: type, impact_level, volatility_impact

7. OrderFlow
   - Market microstructure and order flow dynamics
   - Attributes: type, impact, detectability

8. RiskMetric
   - Risk measures (gap risk, vol-of-vol, correlation risk, etc.)
   - Attributes: definition, measurement, mitigation

9. Position
   - Trading positions and holdings
   - Attributes: type, size, entry_price, current_value

================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP MODEL (10 TYPES)
================================================================================

1. applies_to      - Strategy applies to/effective in Market Regime
2. triggers        - Event triggers Trading Opportunity or Order Flow
3. calculated_from - Greek or Risk Metric calculated from underlying factors
4. constrains      - Risk Metric constrains or limits Position or Strategy
5. affects         - Greek affects Position or Strategy P&L
6. composed_of     - VolSurface composed of or characterized by Greeks
7. creates         - Event creates or impacts Risk Metric
8. indicates       - OrderFlow pattern indicates Trading Opportunity
9. requires        - Strategy requires understanding of specific Greek
10. exposed_to     - Position exposed to specific Market Regime or Risk Factor

All relationships include:
- Confidence score [0, 1]
- Evidence/citation
- Metadata (source, timestamp, etc.)
